      Roadmap to Platinum

      Seven stages, in the order to play them. Story first, strangers second, then activities, collectibles and robberies — and the long grind last. PowerPyx puts the whole run at about 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

        Missables — the honest list

        Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the launch guides, and even that one is unconfirmed. Everything else can be done after the story in free roam. The watch list below covers the story windows worth knowing about anyway. Spoiler protection is on: in the Tracker, story trophies stay hidden until you earn them — uncheck “Hide story spoilers” there to reveal them.

        Watch list — story windows, not locked trophies

        • Chapter 1 optional missions — Get Shirley’s Rum and Steal the Scooter can disappear. PowerPyx says they do not affect trophies or 100% completion.
        • Time-limited optional missions — shown with an hourglass icon. There are a few hundred optional missions and the 100% checklist only needs 100, so a missed one does not matter.
        • Big story choices — keep separate manual saves before them. GTABase advises this as cheap insurance; nothing is confirmed missable.
        • Cheats — using a cheat disables trophies (and saving) for that session, per PowerPyx and GTABase. Do not use cheats on your trophy save.

        Sources & honesty

        The game launched on 19 November 2026 — the day this ledger was built. No complete guide existed yet, so this page says plainly what is known, what is not, and where every fact comes from.

        What we know

        • The list itself — all 55 names, descriptions and grades as PlayStation publishes them, taken from Exophase and PSNProfiles on 18 November 2026 and cross-checked by GTABase and PowerPyx. The two lists agree on every name, description and grade.
        • 55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. 28 of them are hidden on PlayStation until earned. There are no online trophies — every trophy is single-player.
        • One possibly missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude, flagged by both PowerPyx and GTABase because of the Chapter 1 meditation. It is unconfirmed whether that meditation is required.
        • The 100% checklist — 35 requirements, visible only after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Nothing on it is missable; most items need only part of a set.
        • Time and difficulty — PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours. There are no mission gold medals and no mission replay.

        What we do not know yet

        • Seven trophies have no confirmed trigger — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin (PowerPyx: not yet known), plus Personal Best and Actions Speak Louder (story moments, exact scene unconfirmed). Their guides say so instead of inventing a step.
        • Exact locations for most collectibles are still being mapped by the community. This ledger gives counts and menu paths the guides have confirmed, not guessed coordinates.
        • Counts such as 42 shooting-range challenges, 14 drone-race locations and 18 meditation spots come from the launch guides and may be corrected as players verify them in game.
